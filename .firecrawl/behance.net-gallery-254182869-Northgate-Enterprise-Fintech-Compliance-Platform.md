The challenge

Too much information.

Too little clarity.

Compliance teams often work across multiple steps to understand a single client.

Client information, screening results, risk levels and due diligence responses can easily become disconnected.

The solution

The goal wasn't to add more information.

It was to make the right information impossible to miss.

The product was structured around the moments that matter most:

01 — Understand the client

02 — Identify risk

03 — Review evidence

04 — Make a decision
