## Design specificity verdict

**LLM assessment.** Authored in three places: the source-mark system (tier as lightness, mono stamps, tier in the accessible name), the reserved sanction treatment (bar, chip, line, banner, evidence section, refused RFQ), and the striped locked-contact card with a count sentence. The explain-and-fix empty state ("Drop District · 42 remain") is authored copy. Everywhere the buyer acts (RFQ composer, RFQ detail, order detail, settings, thread) the app is category-interchangeable admin-template UI with Geist on it. The ledger thesis holds on the record and the results and disappears the moment the buyer acts.

**Deterministic scan.** Source targets are nearly clean: `app/(app)/app` 0, `components/rfq-create-form.tsx` 0, `components/dashboard` 9 (6 advisory undersized font literals: `marks.tsx:68` 8px/9.5px, `controls.tsx:247` 10px, `photo-tiles.tsx:215` 7px, `rfq-composer.tsx:281` 10px; 3 `broken-image` false positives in tests and a comment), `app/dev/ds` 1 (false positive in a test fixture). The rendered pages tell the real story: Discover 30–34 findings (`nested-cards` ×19, `undersized-ui-text` ×5–8, `clipped-overflow-container` on the shell root, `edge-flush-cards` in the photo strip, `line-length` on 12px captions at ~90–94 chars). Measured: 90 elements with a visible 1px border on Discover, 85 of them inside another bordered element; every button 32px tall with 12/13/14px labels mixed; 42 tab stops before the first result (43 in the table view); the results table's actions column is 4px wide (the `colgroup` sums 994px of a 998px table) so Save/Open/Send RFQ paint over the Type and Workers cells, and the "Registers & certifiers" header runs 53.6px into "Certificates" at every width. Focus styling is complete (a global `:focus-visible` ring, no orphaned `outline: none`); no page-level horizontal overflow at any width.

**Visual overlays.** Script injection succeeded on five pages in the built-in browser and the live detector ran there; those tabs were closed and the live server stopped, so no overlay is on screen now. The console totals are in the scan summary above.

## Overall impression

The engineering under the record pane is at the Linear bar; the surfaces on top of it are not. The reading surfaces (record, results cards, sanction, sources) keep the promise; the table view is broken, the composer is a page reload into a form that knows nothing the record knew, and every detail page is a box of boxes with its own date format. The single biggest opportunity: make the pane the app's one secondary surface and rebuild the list as a ledger grid.

## The founder's four named defects

1. **Database dumps: confirmed.** Table headers overprint and a "dense" row grows to seven lines (`results-table.tsx:24,32,179`); the record dumps 27 raw register strings as chips and eight-line pipe-delimited certificate scopes (`supplier-sheet.tsx:186`, `sheet.tsx:375`); order facts are 14 ungrouped label-over-value rows (`orders/[id]/page.tsx:96`); five date formats. Refuted in part: numbers are tabular, thousands-separated and right-aligned everywhere; page-level empty states are designed. Loading states are bolted on (`discover/loading.tsx:10`).
2. **Hairline overuse: confirmed, with a root cause.** `PageSection` boxes every section by default (`page.tsx:84`); one result card carries ~17 hairline rectangles; 90 bordered elements per Discover viewport, 85 nested. Tone (`canvas`/`surface`/`surface-sunken`) is spent on table headers and the active nav row only.
3. **Buttons look cheap: confirmed.** `default` and `ghost` have no disabled state (`controls.tsx:64,67`), patched by hand at four sites and missed at one (the record's disabled Compare is pixel-identical to the live Save, `sheet.tsx:707`); a 28px size is hand-rolled at six sites; three segmented controls with three active treatments; icon policy arbitrary; 25 brand primaries on one results page.
4. **Composer in the wrong place: confirmed, and worse than a page jump.** Every Send RFQ is a plain `<a>` (no `clientNav`, `controls.tsx:91`), a full document load to `/app/rfqs/new` that discards the search, the selection and the open record; "Back to profile" lands on the full record page; RFQ detail, order detail and the thread are pages and the links between them are page jumps. The gallery's alternative is a 1180px modal over a scrim that also hides the results.

## Cross-cutting verdicts

- **Data grid:** not a system. Two table primitives (44px `DataTable`, 36px `Th/Td`) plus a hand-built third on Products; no header sort, no sticky header, no width discipline, no density control, hover on some tables. Numbers right-aligned and tabular everywhere (good); dates not aligned with them.
- **Buttons:** three variants, one and a half states; 28px hand-rolled; icons arbitrary; three segmented controls; press and focus correct.
- **Surface model:** two non-page surfaces exist (the record pane, the inbox split), both good; everything else is a page or a `<details>`. No drawer, no popover, no confirm primitive; the only dialog is the gallery's dead composer.
- **Hairline/tone:** the tokens have the tools; the components spend them all on borders (`PageSection`, `Tile`, `Stats`, `CertCard`, `PhotoTile`, neutral `Chip`, `default` button, `DetailList`, `OrderFacts`). `selection-bar.tsx:139` hand-types `rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`; two `shadow-sm` on non-floating surfaces.
- **Type and numbers:** faces and weights right; four h1 sizes; three money formatters, no minimum decimals; five date paths, two locale-dependent.
- **States:** empty and error copy is the strongest subsystem; three skeletons match nothing; form errors surface raw HTTP status.
- **Keyboard:** skip link, ⌘K, combobox, pane focus/Escape/return all work; no row navigation, no header sort keys, menus with no keyboard model, tabs are links not a tablist, no ⌘↵, ~100 tab stops per results page.

## Questions to consider
1. If the thesis is receipts, why does every result card carry six photographs of goods the supplier may not make, while the receipts sit in 13px grey tiles?
2. Why is the card the default view of a 10,266-row ledger, and why is the row view the one that is broken?
3. Should a primary "Send RFQ" sit on every row until the composer accepts more than one supplier, or should the one primary live in the pane where it can be honest?
