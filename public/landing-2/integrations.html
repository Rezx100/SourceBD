The ladder

  1. Tier 1Government and RSC

    EPB and RSC. The first word on who a factory is and whether it is safe.

  2. Tier 2Trade bodies

    BGMEA, BKMEA, BTMA and BGAPMEA member registers.

  3. Tier 3Certification bodies

    GOTS, OEKO-TEX, WRAP and SA8000 certificates, with exact dates.

  4. Tier 4Brand supplier lists

    Named in words, only when the brand names that factory.

  5. Tier 5Foreign regulators

    The UFLPA Entity List and sanctions lists. Checked against, never used to fill in a record.
