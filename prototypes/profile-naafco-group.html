About

Self-reported · supplier-verified 2026-03-12

Naafco Group is a vertically integrated knit composite manufacturer based in Tongi, Gazipur, operating since 1998. The group runs spinning, knitting, dyeing, finishing, cutting, and sewing in-house across 4 sister sites in Gazipur and Narayanganj. Specialises in basic and fashion knitwear for European and UK department stores.
