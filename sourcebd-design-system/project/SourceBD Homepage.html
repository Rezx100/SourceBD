How we verify

A source-trust hierarchy, not a proprietary score

We rank every piece of evidence by the authority that issued it. You see the tier, the issuer and the date we last saw it — and decide for yourself.

01

Government Statutory

Statutory authority and ministry-level registers — the strongest primary evidence.

02

Trade association Membership

Industry membership registers — BGMEA, BKMEA, BTMA, BGAPMEA.

03

Certification body Audited

Independent third-party audits — OEKO-TEX, WRAP, GOTS.

04

Brand disclosure Self-reported

Buyer-published supplier lists — attached only when the brand names that specific factory.

05

Regulatory / sanctions Negative screen

UFLPA, OFAC, EU & UK lists — screened to flag, never to endorse.

06

Cross-check only Corroboration

Corroborating signal only — never stands as primary evidence on its own.
