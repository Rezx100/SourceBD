## Home / AI search (`/ai-search`)

"Hi <name>, how can I help you?" over a composer: textarea "Describe anything about product sourcing (drag or paste image)…", attach, mode stops **Supplier search · Product research · Product ideation · More (Product video, Image search, Keyword trends) · Manual filter**, send. Under it a template gallery with tabs All templates / Supplier search / Product research / Product ideation / Product video / Image search; ~36 template prompts ("Source vintage denim suppliers with strong washing capabilities", "Using Export Data to Identify and Source Market-Specific Suppliers", …). Footer: "AI may make mistakes. Check the info as you can."

Conversation behaviour seen:
- A vague prompt ("Denim") gets a **clarifying turn** with four options (Suppliers/Manufacturers · Products · Market trends · Something else) before anything runs.
- A concrete prompt ("Denim jeans manufacturers in Bangladesh") shows "Thought for 3s", then a **results block** titled "Supplier Search: Denim Jeans Manufacturers in Bangladesh" with date, a prose summary ("I found 90 … 68 perfect matches"), a short list of standouts with one-line reasons, and a closing question.
- Three **follow-up suggestion chips** after every answer ("Show detailed profiles … for top 5", "Check Bangladesh denim jeans export shipment data and top buyers", "Research denim jeans market trends …").
- Changing filters in Manual filter posts a user turn "I've updated the filtering requirements. Please assist in re-running the supplier search" followed by the filter chips (🇧🇩 Bangladesh, Asia · Manufacturer · seamless yoga legging scrunch butt · 6114) and re-runs.
- Newer chats (Sep 2026) render the results **inline as a table** in the message: Supplier name · Match score · Product images · Business types · Factory certifications · Employee count · Year founded · Key export market; 8 rows, "1 – 8 of 30", "View 8 more"; the older chats used the separate results block.

## Data behind it (as visible)

Supplier records come from marketplaces (Alibaba, Made-in-China, Global Sources, Thomasnet, Europages) plus **US import bills of lading** (customers, shipments, HS categories, CIF, ports) and marketplace listings (product photos + prices, reviews). Bangladesh suppliers appear with BGMEA membership named in a highlight and with B/L-derived customers; certifications are listed by scheme without number, issuer, expiry or scope. Nothing on the record links to a source page.
