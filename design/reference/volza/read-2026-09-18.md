Read in the built-in browser on the founder's Volza account: **"New Free Trial", plan type Flexible, 10 users, 18–25 Sep 2026**, balance 14 → 13.9 credits after the one search below. ## 1. Coverage — the reason to look

Volza has a **Bangladesh Export – Detailed** database (Bangladesh customs export declarations, **Oct 2019 → Aug 2026**, marked *Premium*), i.e. exporter-side records to **every destination**, not US bills of lading. The country list in the app (`/countrylist`, "585 databases live") groups it under "Detailed Data – With Company Names (125 databases)" with the US import file; UK, Germany, Netherlands, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Sweden, Denmark, Belgium, Canada, Australia, Japan appear only as **"Detailed Mirror"** (Jan 2019 → Sep 2026, derived from partner-country declarations) or "T1" statistical files — the EU/UK side has no customs-level import records of its own, so the Bangladesh export file is where EU/UK buyers come from.

One search run (Bangladesh Export – Detailed, Shipper Name contains "Babylon Garments", Sep 2025 → Aug 2026): **74 shipments · 10 HS codes · 10 consignees · 7 destination countries · 1 port of origin**. Country-of-destination summary (visible in partial view): Germany 203 records $5.57 M · United Kingdom 323 records $4.89 M · Australia 341 records $1.66 M · Japan 21 records $1.05 M · United States 14 records $1.86 M ("records" are declaration lines; "shipments" are declarations). Monthly series showed Sep 2025 → Feb 2026 only, so the newest months are behind the trial's "Preview: latest 6 months" gate. For the same factory ImportYeti holds 437 US sea shipments since 2015 and SourceReady 3,979 merged rows.

The Companies tab holds only saved companies ("5 Full Profile Balance left" on the trial); a company profile is a separate paid unit.

## 3. Prices

- **Website plans** (`/pricing`): Startup **$1,500/yr** (182 countries, *except Premium countries* — Bangladesh is marked Premium, so this tier does not include it; 2 users; 6,000 searches; 360,000 download credits) · SME **$4,500/yr** (200 countries incl. Premium, 10 users, unlimited fair-use searches, 1,040,000 download credits) · Corporate **$9,600/yr**. Data from 2014 on the paid plans; the trial gives "Full view: last 3 years, Preview: latest 6 months". Downloads: Excel/CSV through the app; "Workspace shipment limit" 300k–750k rows.
- **APIs** (`/trade-intelligence-apis/`, docs at docs.volza.com, key by contacting support/sales — no self-serve key): **Bangladesh Export API** `POST https://backend.volza.com/api/v1/bangladesh-exports`, header `X-API-KEY`, **$0.10 per search + $0.05 per shipment record**, filters `hsn_code` or `product_name` (one is mandatory) plus `supplier_name`, `buyer_name`, `destination_country_name`, dates, `max_count_per_page` up to 2,000, sort; returns `api_summary` (count_of_shipments, sum_of_trade_value_usd, count_of_buyers, …), `api_records` (shipment_date, product_description, hsn_code, buyer_name/city/country, supplier_name/city/country, trade_value_usd, gross_weight_kg, unit, origin/destination country and port, shipment_mode) and a `next_page_token`. **Company Transaction Summary API** `GET /api/v1/company-transaction-summary?company_type=supplier&volza_company_id=…` (or by name), Bearer token, **$5 flat per request that returns records (max 10 shipment rows per request), $0.10 when empty**; returns company profile, KPIs, top-10 HS codes / partners / countries / ports, up to 3 years. A Company Lookup API resolves names to Volza ids. Decision-maker contacts API $1.50/record — not for us.
- **Upfront** (docs, Pricing and Rate Limits pages): no minimum published. New API accounts get **100 free requests including record fees, no card**; then card or bank transfer billed monthly on usage, or **prepaid credits with no stated minimum** (non-refundable, never expire); out of credits → HTTP 402. Standard plan: 50 requests/min, **2 years of history**; Custom plan: 150/min, 7 years. Pricing values are "configurable" — 30 days' notice, 90 days grandfathered. The key itself comes from helpdesk@volza.com, so confirm "no minimum" in that exchange.
- Worked cost for one supplier through the export API: 1 search + 100 rows = **$5.10**; 74 rows (Babylon, one year) = $3.80. The summary API at $5 gives the top-10 lists but only 10 rows.

## 4. Terms (`/term-of-use/`, read 18 Sep 2026)

The website subscription is unusable for SourceBD: *"Subscription to Volza's database is strictly private and confidential, and usage is restricted to self-use only"*; content "solely for your internal informational purposes. No redistribution, transmission, or other forms of sharing"; no derivative products, no "framing … with the aim of developing a rival product"; no scraping of any kind; "Subscription plans are not available to resellers"; market-research firms need a separate "Market Research Plan"; governing law Dubai, UAE. — https://www.volza.com/term-of-use/

The API page is the product sold for embedding ("Embed … into your platform … directly into your CRM, ERP, or product"), but its contract is not published — the docs say "Contact the Volza support team to obtain your API token" (helpdesk@volza.com) and pricing "can be adjusted at any time". Whether stored rows may be shown to SourceBD buyers has to be in that agreement in writing before any money moves.

## 5. Verdict for the handoff §4.8

- **Data:** Volza's Bangladesh export file is the only source seen that answers all three gaps at once — buyers in the **EU/UK/Australia/Japan as well as the US**, **key export markets** (Country of Destination with FOB value), and HS lines with unit rates. ImportYeti answers US only.
- **Contract:** not the $1,500–$4,500 subscription (self-use only). Only the API, under a written agreement that allows display to our buyers; price ≈ $5 per supplier unlock either way, so the same on-demand design and the same `provider_spend` row work with `provider = 'volza'`. The schema in §4.8 already fits: `supplier_export_markets` fills from `destination_country`, `supplier_customers` from `buyer_name`, `supplier_shipments` from `api_records` with `cif_usd` ← `trade_value_usd` (FOB, label it so).
- **Match key:** `supplier_name` string (plus Company Lookup id once known); same `matched_how` honesty as ImportYeti.
- **Open:** the API agreement's redistribution clause; whether the trial's 6-month preview gap means a 6-month lag on the API too (docs say "To 31-Aug-2026", the workspace chart stopped at Feb 2026 — ask).
